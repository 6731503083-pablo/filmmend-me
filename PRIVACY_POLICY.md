## Information We Collect

- **Account information:** email address and display name when you register or sign in.

## How We Use Information

- To create and manage your account.
- To sync your watchlist across devices.

## Third-Party Services

The App uses Firebase Authentication and Firestore for account and data storage. These services process data on our behalf. The App also communicates with the TMDB API to fetch movie data. Filmmend Me is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share your personal data with third parties for advertising.

## Data Retention

We keep your account and watchlist data while your account is active. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us.

## Your Choices
